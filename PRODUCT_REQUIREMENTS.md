# Product Requirements

## Product promise

Private, supportive recovery without judgment. The primary navigation is Home, Track, Rescue, Insights, and Profile, with Rescue as the central action.

## First-release programs

The canonical taxonomy is defined by `StandardPrograms`; screens render program definitions rather than implementing category-specific forks.

## Safety

Tier C programs must never produce detox plans, taper schedules, medication advice, diagnoses, sudden-stop safety claims, or automated treatment. Safety resources, Rescue, deletion, and essential privacy are never paywalled.

## Accounts

Users may continue privately, authenticate with Google, or use email passwordless authentication. Guest records upload only after explicit consent.

## Implemented application shell

First launch presents the shared program taxonomy and activates the selected program locally. The five-tab shell exposes Home, Track, Rescue, Insights, and Profile. Home provides the next supportive action and tier-aware recovery guidance. Track supports activity, urge, craving, slip, quantity, duration, cost, intensity, and common trigger entries.

Profile exposes optional Android Usage Access with transparent capability limits. When granted, the app estimates daily app time, foreground openings, sessions, rapid reopenings, morning activity, and late-night activity.

## Rescue

The offline Rescue flow enforces a 60-second pause, breathing guidance, personal motivation, initial urge intensity, trigger selection, three relevant replacement actions, final urge intensity, and a locally recorded outcome. Completion language preserves progress after slips and difficult moments. Medically high-risk programs show professional and emergency escalation without treatment, detox, or taper instructions.

## Insights

Seven-day insights are calculated locally from recorded events. They show check-in and slip counts, average recorded intensity, trend direction, most recorded trigger, most active risk period, and Rescue outcomes. Each result includes a direct explanation of the records used and is explicitly presented as a pattern rather than a diagnosis or prediction.
