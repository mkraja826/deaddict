# Google Play content-rating and target-audience guidance

This document records the intended DeAddict launch answers. The Play Console questionnaire remains the source of the final rating, and every production coaching phrase, program description, screenshot, and store-listing translation must be reviewed before submission.

## Product classification

- App type: application, not a game.
- Primary category recommendation: Health & Fitness.
- Core purpose: self-management support for habits and addictions.
- The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, prescribe, provide detox instructions, or provide taper instructions.
- The app does not facilitate buying, selling, manufacturing, or consuming controlled substances.
- User recovery records are private and are not published to a social feed.

## Expected questionnaire areas

### Violence, sexual content, gambling, and fear

The intended release contains no graphic violence, sexual content, gambling, horror, or realistic threats. Store assets must remain suitable for a general Play Store surface even when the app itself is targeted to adults.

### Controlled substances and addiction themes

The application discusses substance and behavioral addictions in a recovery context. Questionnaire answers must disclose references to alcohol, nicotine, drugs, medication misuse, gambling, pornography, and other supported programs where those references appear in production content. The presentation is preventive and recovery-oriented and must never glamorize or instruct harmful use.

### Strong language

Rook coaching can offer direct or banter-oriented tones. Before release, scan every production phrase and localization. If any phrase contains profanity, insults, or other strong language, answer the rating questionnaire accurately. Safety warnings, medically high-risk flows, crisis language, onboarding, store copy, and notifications must never use abusive language.

### User interaction and user-generated content

Users can enter private notes and recovery information, but the current release has no public posting, direct messaging, public profile, or user-to-user content exchange. Private user-entered text is not moderated as public content because it remains device-local.

### Location and purchases

The application does not require precise location. Optional subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing and must be disclosed as in-app purchases.

## Target audience recommendation

Launch as an adult-focused recovery application. Do not select children as a target audience, do not use child-directed imagery, and do not market DeAddict as a school or child-health product without a separate legal, safety, clinical, and policy review.

## Final review checklist

1. Export all Rook phrases, notifications, program names, onboarding text, Rescue text, and safety messages.
2. Search every supported language for profanity, sexual wording, drug-use instructions, self-harm content, and unqualified medical claims.
3. Confirm store screenshots use fictional data and do not depict minors.
4. Confirm the app category, target audience, health-app declarations, and in-app purchase declaration in Play Console.
5. Save the completed rating questionnaire and resulting certificate with the 1.0.0 release record.
