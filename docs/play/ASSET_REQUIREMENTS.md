# Google Play asset requirements and DeAddict capture plan

Use this document for the 1.0.0 Asset Library upload. Every image must reflect the current production build, use fictional recovery information, avoid medical-outcome claims, and remain readable on small screens.

## Required and recommended assets

### App icon

- 512 × 512 pixels.
- 32-bit PNG with alpha.
- Maximum file size: 1024 KB.
- No price, ranking, Google Play badge, misleading certification, or tiny text.
- Keep the central Rook/DeAddict mark readable after Play applies its mask.

### Feature graphic

- 1024 × 500 pixels.
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- Keep important content near the center and avoid fine detail.
- Recommended message: **One app. Every Recovery Track.**
- Do not repeat a large app icon, show a cure claim, or use before/after health imagery.

### Phone screenshots

Google Play requires at least two screenshots across device types. For stronger merchandising eligibility, prepare at least four portrait phone screenshots at 1080 × 1920 pixels using a 9:16 ratio. PNG or JPEG only; no alpha.

Recommended 1.0.0 sequence:

1. **Multiple Recovery Tracks** — Tracks screen with a primary and supporting track using fictional names/data.
2. **One daily check-in** — Today screen showing shared context and independent track outcomes.
3. **Rescue in difficult moments** — guided pause, trigger, replacement action, and urge re-check.
4. **Goal-aware Insights** — selected-track progress with no combined recovery score.
5. **Private by design** — biometric lock, screenshot protection, local notes, and deletion controls.
6. **Choose your coaching tone** — safe representation of Direct and supportive tone settings; never show abusive copy in the listing.

### Large-screen assets

If tablets or Chromebooks are included in supported device distribution or store marketing, prepare at least four accurate large-screen screenshots. Do not upload stretched phone captures. Complete the large-screen quality review before advertising tablet support.

## Localization

Provide separate screenshot sets when promotional text is embedded in images. At minimum, prepare English assets. Hindi and Telugu listings should receive localized screenshots before those store listings are actively promoted. Do not rely on one image containing multiple languages.

## Fictional test-data standard

- No real name, email, account identifier, purchase token, support ticket, or recovery history.
- Use neutral fictional tracks such as Gaming, Caffeine, Social Media, or Late-night Browsing.
- Avoid presenting controlled-substance amounts or withdrawal instructions in promotional images.
- Keep urge ratings and outcomes realistic but non-sensational.
- Disable notification previews that could reveal sensitive text during capture.

## Visual quality checklist

- Capture from the release build with production typography and theme.
- Review light mode and dark mode; choose one coherent listing set.
- No debug banners, test tags, loading spinners, errors, clipped content, or keyboard overlays.
- Check 320 dpi and 420 dpi devices.
- Keep captions concise and policy-safe.
- Verify feature names match the final app exactly.
- Preserve enough contrast and text size for accessibility.
- Store source files separately from upload-ready exports.
