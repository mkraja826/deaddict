# DeAddict Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 July 2026

DeAddict is a local-first recovery support application that helps people manage one or more Recovery Tracks, record daily check-ins, use urge-management tools, and review progress. This policy describes the information processed by the Android application and its supporting services. It must be published at the public privacy-policy URL used in Google Play before production release.

## Information processed

### Information stored on the device

DeAddict may store Recovery Tracks, goals, daily outcomes, mood, stress, energy, sleep quality, urge ratings, triggers, replacement actions, Rescue sessions, notification settings, coaching preferences, and privacy settings on the device. Private check-in notes are device-local and are excluded from cloud-sync payloads.

Optional app-usage monitoring uses Android usage-access permission. Usage information is processed locally to support user-selected limits and is not uploaded by the current release. Biometric authentication is performed by Android; DeAddict does not receive or store fingerprint, face, or device-credential templates.

The app records aggregate local reliability counters such as app starts, previous-crash markers, and success, failure, or slow-operation counts. These counters contain no owner identifiers, Recovery Track identifiers, addiction types, notes, triggers, outcomes, exception messages, or stack traces, and are not uploaded in the current release.

### Information processed when an account is used

Creating an account is optional. When a user signs in, the authentication provider and Supabase may process an account identifier, email address, authentication tokens, and security metadata needed to operate the account. Eligible recovery records may be synchronized so the user can restore them or use them across devices. Private notes remain excluded from synchronization.

### Purchases

Google Play Billing processes subscription purchases. DeAddict may receive a purchase token, product identifier, entitlement status, and related verification result to confirm access to paid features. Payment-card details are handled by Google Play and are not received by DeAddict.

## Purposes

Information is processed to provide the features requested by the user, preserve and synchronize recovery records, authenticate accounts, verify subscriptions, schedule reminders, protect the app with optional privacy controls, prevent abuse, troubleshoot reliability, and comply with deletion requests.

## Service providers and sharing

DeAddict may use Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, and Supabase authentication, database, and server-function services. Information is shared with these providers only as needed to deliver authentication, synchronization, deletion, or purchase verification. DeAddict does not sell personal data and does not include an advertising network in the current release.

## Security

The Android application disables cleartext network traffic and device backup for app data. Network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to synchronized records is restricted by authenticated ownership rules. Users may enable biometric/device-credential locking and screenshot protection. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so users should also secure their devices and accounts.

## User choices

Users may use core local features without creating an account. Optional permissions, notifications, usage monitoring, analytics preferences, biometric locking, and screenshot protection can be controlled in the app. Local recovery data can be deleted from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local data subject to Android platform behavior.

## Account and data deletion

Signed-in users have an in-app account-deletion path in the You/account area. A public external deletion-request page must also be linked from the Google Play listing. A verified deletion request removes the DeAddict account and associated synchronized recovery data, subject only to limited retention that may be required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Local data on other devices must be removed from those devices by using the in-app local deletion control or uninstalling the app.

## Retention

Local information remains until the user deletes it, deletes app data, or uninstalls the app. Synchronized information remains while the account is active and is removed following a verified deletion request, except for information that a service provider or law requires to be retained for a limited purpose. Google Play may retain transaction records under its own policies.

## Children

DeAddict is not designed for children who cannot legally consent to account creation or data processing in their jurisdiction. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided account information should use the public support or deletion channel shown on the Google Play listing.

## Health and safety

DeAddict is a self-management support tool, not a medical device, emergency service, diagnosis, or treatment provider. It does not provide detoxification or tapering instructions. People who may be in immediate danger or experiencing severe withdrawal symptoms should contact local emergency services or a qualified medical professional.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected in this document and its effective date. The public developer name, support email, support website, privacy-policy URL, and account-deletion URL shown on the DeAddict Google Play listing are the official contact channels for privacy questions and requests.
