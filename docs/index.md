# DeAddict Support and Policies

DeAddict is a local-first Android recovery-support application for managing one or more independent Recovery Tracks.

## Public documents

- [Privacy Policy](legal/PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
- [Account and Data Deletion](legal/ACCOUNT_DELETION.md)
- [Terms of Service](legal/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md)

## Support

The verified support email and developer website shown on the DeAddict Google Play listing are the official contact channels. Before publication, enable a public HTTPS deployment for this `docs` directory and configure the resulting privacy, deletion, terms, and support URLs in the manual publish-readiness workflow and Play Console.

## Safety

DeAddict is a self-management support tool, not a medical device, emergency service, diagnosis, or treatment provider. It does not provide detoxification or tapering instructions. If you may be in immediate danger or have severe withdrawal symptoms, contact local emergency services or a qualified medical professional.
