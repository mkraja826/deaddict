Official public resources

Private recovery support, with clear boundaries.

DeAddict helps adults manage multiple habits through independent Recovery Tracks, daily check-ins, Rescue tools, and cautious insights. This site explains how the app handles data and how to get support.

Safety: DeAddict is a self-management tool. It is not emergency care, diagnosis, treatment, detox guidance, or taper guidance. For immediate danger, overdose risk, or severe withdrawal symptoms, contact local emergency services or a qualified medical professional.

Privacy by design

Private notes remain device-local. Recovery Tracks keep separate progress histories. DeAddict does not create one combined addiction or recovery score, and it does not present observed associations as proof that one behaviour caused another.
