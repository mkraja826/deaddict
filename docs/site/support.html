Official support center

Get help with DeAddict

Contact the public support address for account, synchronization, billing, privacy, deletion, or accessibility problems. Do not include private notes, passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary recovery details.

Email DeAddict support

Information that helps

  • App version and Android version.
  • Device model and whether the problem occurs on Wi-Fi, mobile data, or offline.
  • The affected feature, such as sign-in, sync, daily check-in, Insights, Rescue, notifications, or billing.
  • A short description of the expected and actual result.
  • A screenshot only when it does not reveal sensitive recovery information.

Account and synchronization

Sign-in is optional. If synchronization is unavailable, keep the app installed and avoid repeatedly creating duplicate accounts. Local pending records are designed to remain authoritative until synchronization can resume. Support may ask for non-sensitive timing and version details, but should never ask for your password or one-time authentication code.

Billing

Purchases are processed by Google Play. First use the app’s Restore purchases action and confirm that the same Google Play account is active. Refund eligibility and transaction history are managed under Google Play rules. DeAddict support may help investigate entitlement verification but cannot see full payment-card details.

Privacy and deletion

Private notes remain device-local. Deleting local recovery data is separate from deleting a signed-in cloud account. Review the privacy policy and account-deletion instructions before requesting deletion.

Accessibility

Reports about TalkBack labels, focus order, large text, Switch Access, contrast, touch targets, or motion are welcome. Include the accessibility service and text-size setting you were using. Avoid attaching recovery content unless it is essential to reproduce the issue.

Safety and emergencies

Support email is not monitored as an emergency service. For immediate danger, overdose risk, severe withdrawal symptoms, or a mental-health emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
