Privacy policy

DeAddict is designed as a local-first recovery self-management app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Information you choose to provide

The app may process Recovery Track selections, goals, check-ins, mood, stress, energy, sleep quality, recorded outcomes, measured values, urge ratings, triggers, Rescue actions, reminder preferences, coaching-tone preferences, and optional private notes. You control whether and when these records are created.

Local-first storage

Recovery data is stored on your Android device first. Private per-track notes are device-local and are excluded from cloud sync payloads and aggregate diagnostics. Device security features such as biometric lock and screenshot protection are optional controls you can enable.

Optional account and cloud sync

Sign-in is optional. When you sign in and use synchronization, the app may send account identifiers and eligible recovery records to the configured Supabase service so your supported records can be restored across devices. Cloud access is protected by authenticated row-level security. Private notes are not sent to Supabase.

Daily check-ins are reconciled using owner, date, Recovery Track, revision, and timestamp information. This is used to prevent accidental duplication and resolve cross-device changes. It is not used to create a universal recovery score.

Android permissions and device information

Notification permission is used only for reminders you enable. Optional usage-access permission can estimate app time and opening counts for supported digital habits. DeAddict does not read message content, keystrokes, browser history, or the content displayed inside other apps.

Billing

If you purchase DeAddict Plus, Google Play processes payment information. DeAddict receives purchase and entitlement information needed to verify and restore access. The app does not receive your full card or bank details.

Diagnostics

The current app records privacy-safe aggregate health counters locally, such as app starts, previous-crash markers, check-in save success or failure, Insights success or failure, and slow-operation counts. These records do not include stack traces, exception messages, owner identifiers, Recovery Track identifiers, addiction types, outcomes, triggers, or private notes. This phase does not upload those local health counters.

How information is used

  • Provide daily check-ins, Rescue tools, reminders, goal-aware progress, and cautious Insights.
  • Keep Recovery Tracks and their histories independent.
  • Restore eligible cloud records when signed in.
  • Verify subscriptions and protect account access.
  • Maintain app reliability without collecting private recovery content in diagnostics.

Sharing and sale

DeAddict does not sell personal information or recovery data. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate optional authentication, synchronization, billing, and app distribution, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards. Data may also be disclosed when legally required.

Retention and deletion

Local recovery data remains on the device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or use the relevant deletion control. Cloud records remain until deleted through the in-app account deletion flow or an accepted external deletion request, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or completed financial records.

Deleting local recovery data is separate from deleting a cloud account. The app explains the scope before either action. External instructions are available on the account-deletion page.

Children

DeAddict is intended for adults and is not directed to children. The Google Play target-audience declaration should remain aligned with this product position.

International use

Data may be processed in locations where the selected service providers operate. Users are responsible for reviewing local requirements. Region-specific rights may apply, including rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal information.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating this page and its effective date. The policy shown in Google Play and the app must point to the same current public version.
