Account and data deletion

Delete your DeAddict account

You can permanently delete a signed-in account inside the app. An external request path is also available without reinstalling the app or signing in.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open DeAddict and unlock it when biometric protection is enabled.
  2. Open the You tab.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the scope and confirm Delete permanently.

The in-app action is intended to delete the cloud account, eligible cloud recovery records, and recovery data stored on that device. It is separate from the local-only deletion control.

Request deletion without the app

Send an email from the address associated with your DeAddict account. Use the subject “DeAddict account deletion request”. Do not include private notes, passwords, authentication codes, or detailed recovery history.

Request account deletion

Support may ask you to complete a limited identity-verification step before deletion. This is used to prevent unauthorized deletion and should not require sharing a password or one-time authentication code.

Data included

An accepted account-deletion request covers the authentication account and cloud recovery data associated with that account, including eligible Recovery Tracks, goal versions, synchronized check-ins, synchronized outcomes, and synchronization metadata.

Data that may remain

  • Private notes stored only on a device cannot be removed remotely. Delete local recovery data or uninstall the app on each device.
  • Google Play purchase and transaction records are controlled by Google and applicable financial-retention rules.
  • Limited security, fraud-prevention, legal, or audit records may be retained where required by law or necessary to demonstrate that deletion occurred.
  • De-identified aggregate information that cannot reasonably identify an individual may be retained.

Local data only

To remove recovery data from one device without deleting a cloud account, use You → Your data → Delete local recovery data. This removes local Recovery Tracks, check-ins, Rescue sessions, and local Insights from that device. It does not by itself delete a signed-in cloud account.

Timing and confirmation

Deletion requests are processed after identity verification and review of legal retention requirements. A confirmation will be sent to the requesting address when processing is complete or when additional action is required.
